# StockSOS: investor and revenue readiness

## Current position

StockSOS is a worldwide B2B sourcing marketplace with supplier listings, buyer SOS requests, supplier matching, quotations, orders, and protected account roles. The code also contains supplier plan records, a manual Reach sharing workflow, and a paid-gated sales assistant foundation. Paid subscriptions and live checkout are not available yet. No revenue, traction, investment commitment, or valuation is asserted by this document.

An informal statement of interest in a USD 200 million investment is not a financing agreement. The business is not registered yet. The founder and counsel should verify the investor and agree on legal structure, jurisdiction, security terms, valuation, ownership, governance, and staged funding before any commitment or sensitive disclosure.

## Product thesis

Build a **permission-based sourcing network**: a buyer describes a need once; StockSOS structures the request, finds verified stock, routes it to suitable suppliers, collects comparable quotations, and tracks fulfilment. Suppliers see demand they can serve and receive tools to respond quickly. The advantage should come from accurate inventory, supplier reliability, and completed transactions that improve matching over time. No competitor-proof claim is possible or necessary.

## Revenue paths to validate

| Revenue path | Customer value | Launch condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supplier subscriptions | Faster responses, analytics, team tools, and verified distribution workflows | Billing, entitlements, invoicing, and cancellation working end to end |
| Marketplace transaction fee | Buyers and suppliers complete a protected order | Registered entity, suitable licensed payment provider, clear fees, refunds, disputes, and payout controls |
| Optional promoted listings | Suppliers pay for clearly labeled placement | Demand and attribution data, explicit supplier budget consent, ad policy review |
| Enterprise procurement/API | Larger buyers automate sourcing and reporting | Proven service quality, contracts, access controls, and integration support |

Do not count order value as StockSOS revenue. Do not promise investors a distribution or return from hypothetical sales. Pricing and take rates should be tested with real buyers and suppliers before financial forecasts are presented.

## Build sequence

1. **Measure the real funnel.** The admin Growth page now separates requests, supplier matches, quotations, orders, completed orders, and provider-verified payments. It suppresses payment volume if the record set is incomplete and never mixes currencies.
2. **Prove repeatable sourcing.** Run a defined pilot in selected product categories and locations. Measure request fill rate, median time to first quote, accepted quotes, completed orders, repeat buyers, and disputes. Use actual records only.
3. **Improve supplier conversion.** Add opt-in response templates, stock-aware opportunity alerts, and assistant responses constrained by current listing and delivery terms. Keep a human confirmation boundary around price changes and external messages.
4. **Enable protected commerce.** After registration and provider onboarding, test checkout, payment verification, delivery evidence, refund handling, and supplier payouts in staging. Use a licensed provider rather than holding buyer funds directly.
5. **Expand distribution.** Connect official search and social APIs only with platform approval and supplier consent. Measure attributable leads and sales before charging for promotion.
6. **Scale the data layer.** Replace the first-release direct payment read limit with a reviewed aggregate database function before transaction volume exceeds 1,000 verified payments in 30 days.

## Before commercial launch or an investment round

- Register the operator and confirm its jurisdiction, ownership, bank account, tax status, and legal contacts with counsel.
- Have counsel review supplier/buyer terms, privacy policy, refunds, payments, cross-border data handling, and the investment documents.
- Move production hosting off Vercel Hobby before commercial use; its current terms restrict Hobby to personal or non-commercial use: https://vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby
- Select a payment provider based on the registered entity's country and supported supplier payout countries. Do not enable live checkout merely because an API key exists.
- Prepare a diligence room with source ownership, security test results, architecture, migration history, real operating metrics, customer references, cap table, and audited or accountant-prepared financials when available. Keep credentials out of it.
- Ask the investor for the identity of the investing entity, source-of-funds process, written indicative terms, decision timeline, and a direct discussion with counsel. Do not pay an upfront "release" or "processing" fee to receive an investment.
